# Phishing Email Analysis and Investigation

## 1. Project Overview
This project documents my phishing email analysis learning and practice as part of my SOC Analyst development.

## 2. Objective
The objective is to learn how to examine suspicious emails, identify potential phishing indicators, and document an evidence-based assessment.

## 3. Investigation Areas
Depending on the evidence available, the investigation may include:
- Sender address and display name
- Email subject and message content
- Email headers and authentication results
- Suspicious URLs and link destinations
- Unexpected or suspicious attachments
- Social engineering techniques, such as urgency or impersonation

## 4. Investigation Process
1. Reviewed the available email information.
2. Identified potential indicators of phishing.
3. Examined relevant headers, links, or attachments where available and safe.
4. Assessed the evidence and considered alternative explanations.
5. Documented the assessment and recommended next steps.
